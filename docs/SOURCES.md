The upstream license grants cover their compilations, not a blanket sublicense of linked articles,
company trademarks, or unrelated website material. Article bodies are not stored or redistributed.
Review summaries are short original factual paraphrases, with URLs and review dates. Source opinions
remain attributed claims, not adjudicated facts, especially allegations or causal explanations.

as audit artifacts. Exact source snapshot SHA-256 hashes are in exports/checksums.json.

suitability was not established in this collection pass, not that permission can never be obtained.
